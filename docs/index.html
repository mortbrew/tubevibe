▶ tubevibe

YouTube, your subscriptions and your local videos and music, in the terminal. Tabs, Watch Later, instant search and video embedded in kitty.

Based on tubetui by polblancoo. Free and open source · MIT / Apache-2.0 · Linux, Windows and macOS.

Features

🏠 Home and Subscriptions

Your feed, what's new on your channels, Discover and My Channels. Shorts are left out.

🔎 Search in a Second

20 results and 20 more as you scroll, with a preview, thumbnail, length and size. All of YouTube, your channels or inside one.

🎬 Video Your Way

In an mpv window (the default), embedded in kitty, or laid over the app on X11. Quality and codec change on the fly.

🗂 Tabs

One video per tab; the sound keeps playing while you browse. Alt+, and Alt+. switch tabs with one hand.

🕒 Watch Later and Downloads

w saves to YouTube's Watch Later, l to tubevibe's, d downloads with subtitles and Downloads shows the progress.

📜 History and Resume

Everything you watched, in tubevibe and, logged in, on YouTube too. A video opens again where you left it.

⏩ Chapters and SponsorBlock

[ ] jump chapters; sponsors and "subscribe!" reminders are skipped by themselves.

🎧 Audio Only

a plays a video as sound only, and goes back to the video at the same second.

💬 Subtitles and Dubbing

Original, subtitles or dubbed audio, in the language you choose, without stopping the video.

🔐 Your Login, No Passwords

Uses your browser's login (Brave, Chrome, Firefox, Edge…) or a cookies.txt.

📼 Your Files

Your local videos and music with a play queue. Mark several and send them to the trash.

✨ Light and Themed

About 25 MB of memory and starts at once. Follows your Omarchy theme. English and Spanish.

Install

Every release on GitHub Releases has builds for Linux, macOS and Windows. All of them need mpv and yt-dlp. On Linux or macOS the installer picks the right file:

curl -sSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mortbrew/tubevibe/main/install.sh | sh

From source, anywhere Rust 1.88+ runs:

cargo install --locked --git https://github.com/mortbrew/tubevibe

To watch the video inside the app use kitty; in any other terminal it opens in its own window.
